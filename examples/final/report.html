Procurement, allocation and system cost

Each method is evaluated on the same scenario paths, supply-chain dynamics, action caps and cost coefficients. HAPPO and MAPPO jointly select platform orders, SME allocation and supplier replenishment. The third comparator is a causal rolling base-stock heuristic with gap-based allocation.

Branch bullwhip

For each supplier, branch bullwhip is the variance of its replenishment orders divided by the variance of platform orders allocated to that supplier, as in Eq. (8). A zero denominator gives N/A, not zero.

Evaluation settings

Holding-cost coefficients: [100, 20, 20]; backlog-cost coefficients: [300, 200, 200], in platform, LE, SME order. Lead times: [1, 2, 0]. Platform/supplier order caps: 220 / 220. HAPPO alpha=0.2; MAPPO alpha=0.7. Reward weights affect policy training; all methods are scored with the same system-cost objective.
